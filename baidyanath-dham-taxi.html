Baba Baidyanath Dham – The Sacred Jyotirlinga

Baba Baidyanath Dham in Deoghar, Jharkhand is one of the twelve sacred Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva. Every year, millions of devotees visit this holy temple for darshan and blessings. Kailashpati Tour & Travels makes your pilgrimage comfortable with reliable taxi service from any pickup point to the temple and surrounding sacred sites.

Our Baidyanath Dham Taxi Services

  • Airport / Railway Pickup — We pick you up from Deoghar Airport or Jasidih Junction and take you directly to Baidyanath Dham
  • Local Darshan Package — Visit Baidyanath Dham along with nearby temples including Naulakha Mandir, Satsang Ashram and Tapovan
  • Return Journey — Drop-off to airport, railway station or outstation destination after darshan
  • Multi-Day Pilgrimage — Combine Baidyanath Dham with Tarapith, Rajrappa or other religious destinations
  • Sawan Special — Special arrangements during Shravan month for Kanwar Yatra and peak pilgrimage season
